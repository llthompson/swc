What to Expect

No one likes being the new person in a room, so we want you to feel welcome from the start. Every meeting starts with a short round of intros: name, pronouns, and what type of writing you enjoy. We are all here to build community, and every person who walks in the door helps us do that.

After intros, the main activity follows a different format depending on which Saturday of the month it is.

Sapphic Writers Circle

Our Saturday Lineup

A different format every week

1st Saturday Hype Squad Bring a short piece of writing to share (under 1000 words).
2nd Saturday Play Write Writing games and exercises, fun prompts, flash fiction, and more.
3rd Saturday Workshop 4 U Sign up in advance to have your writing workshopped by the group.
4th Saturday Zine Scene Collaborate on our quarterly zine: brainstorm, write, and create together.
5th Saturday Side Craft Bring Your Own Craft (like the kind with glue sticks and glitter) for an extra creative Saturday.
