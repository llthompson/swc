the sapphic scholastic: an indie book fair in ATX

Free to attend · Hosted by Sapphic Writers Circle

  • Jan 16, 2027 Saturday
  • 11:00 AM until 2:00 PM
  • 2700 W Anderson Ln Austin, TX 78757 at Epoch Coffee - Village

Spend a Saturday with Austin's sapphic authors! Hosted by Sapphic Writers Circle, the sapphic scholastic brings together local sapphic writers of every genre: fiction, nonfiction, poetry, graphic novels, and everything in between. Browse the tables, find your next favorite read (or five), and meet the people who wrote them. It's free to attend, so bring your friends!

Thank you to Epoch Coffee at the Village for hosting us in their space, and to Family Eldercare and Rainbow Connections for their continued support in providing a safe meeting space for us.

Are You a Queer Author?

We're looking for sapphic authors in the greater Austin area to table at the fair. There is no fee to participate. Any genre, traditionally or self-published. Applications close November 27th.

Who We Are

Sapphic Writers Circle is a free community for sapphic writers in Austin. We meet every Saturday to write, share our work, and cheer each other on. All genres and experience levels welcome.
